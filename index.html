Your Romantic Journey

A carefully curated 8-day itinerary designed for love and adventure.

1
17 May 2026

Arrival in Paradise

Krabi (Ao Nang Beach)
Flight Inc.

Fly from Mumbai to Krabi. Meet your Veena World Tour Manager and transfer to your hotel. Evening visit to the stunning Ao Nang Beach for a romantic sunset.

2
18 May 2026

4-Island Adventure

Krabi → Phuket

Exciting island hopping tour: Phra Nang Cave, Tup Island, Chicken Island, and Poda Island. Later, ferry/transfer to Phuket. Evening explore Patong Beach nightlife.

3
19 May 2026

James Bond Island

Phuket (Phang Nga Bay)

Visit Ao Phang Nga National Park and the famous Needle Rock (007 movie). Evening spectacular Fantasea or Siam Niramit cultural show.

4
20 May 2026

City Lights & Cabaret

Phuket → Bangkok → Pattaya
Flight Inc.

Fly to Bangkok, then drive to Pattaya. Check into hotel. Evening witness the world-famous Alcazar Cabaret Show with dazzling costumes.

5
21 May 2026

Coral Island & Gardens

Pattaya

Speedboat to Coral Island (Parasailing included). Visit Nong Nooch Village (Botanical Garden & Elephant Show). Evening Scientific Thai Massage.

6
22 May 2026

Sky High & Shopping

Pattaya → Bangkok

Visit Pattaya Tower (Speed Shuttle). En route to Bangkok, visit Gems Gallery. Evening shopping at Indra Square.

7
23 May 2026

Safari & Dinner Cruise

Bangkok

Visit Safari World & Marine Park (Dolphin Show, Spy War). Evening romantic Dinner Cruise on Chao Phraya River.

8
24 May 2026

Golden Buddha & Farewell

Bangkok → Mumbai

City orientation tour including 'Wat Pho' (Reclining Buddha). Proceed to airport for flight back to Mumbai.
